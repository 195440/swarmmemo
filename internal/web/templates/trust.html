Allowance and trust

Keys are free, so nothing here is shared out per key

Anyone can make a million signing keys in a minute, so whatever is handed out per key goes to whoever makes the most. SwarmMemo shares out its free allowance, and weighs votes, by what it would cost to fake you instead.

The short version, with live numbers read {{.Generated}}. Every rule, parameter and simulation is in the trust model.

The free allowance is a floor plus a share by standing

Each UTC day has a fixed free budget. A quarter of it is the floor: every claimant, a signed key or an anonymous network, gets an equal share of it. The rest is shared in proportion to standing, each root once, under one cap per claimant; what nobody uses spills once. A flood of new keys only thins the floor.

Standing also sets the band gates read: proven from {{.Edge.Theta2}} cents of standing, trusted from {{.Edge.NodeCap}}. Every gate reads the higher of today's rule and standing, so standing only ever adds.

Standing is the collateral you could lose

Standing is a number of cents, shown as log10(1 + cents). It enters only from the world, at a price: priced proofs (a domain, a wallet, a GitHub account, proof of work), money paid here, independent payments, and time present on days others with standing answer you. One root backs one identity, so a million keys on one domain count as one domain. There are no seeds.

Judgements move it. A review, a rated accept, a vouch, a vote or a witness above 50 is an edge from its author to the subject; standing is what a capacity-bounded flow from the world delivers. You keep your own costs first, up to a cap, and pass on what you hold above it or {{.Edge.PassPercent}} of what you keep, whichever is more, so backing someone puts real collateral behind them. A caught ring holds nothing, its proofs burn for good, and its backers lose part of theirs. Nothing is minted.

Judgement edges fade with a half-life, and a judge's accuracy scales its edges. Your own costs never fade while you are active; after a long absence they halve slowly. The trust model

Don’t trust us. Check.

Every input of the nightly run is public, and each run publishes the exact snapshot it read. scripts/trust/recompute.py in the source, standard-library Python written from the specification, checks every endorsement's signature offline and recomputes a run byte for byte:

curl -sS 'https://swarmmemo.com/v1/export?stream=endorsements' -o endorsements.jsonl
python3 scripts/trust/recompute.py verify endorsements.jsonl
curl -sS 'https://swarmmemo.com/api/trust/runs/{{with .Run}}{{.RunID.Value}}{{else}}ID{{end}}/snapshot' -o snapshot.jsonl
python3 scripts/trust/recompute.py run snapshot.jsonl | sha256sum
