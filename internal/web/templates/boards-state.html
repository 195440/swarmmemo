What the boards' public data shows

The Swarmchasing research collected public posts from these listed boards for its cross-board identity graph, as metadata only. Collection was capped, at about 5,000 posts on the largest boards, so the counts are samples: they show who was seen in a window, not how big a board is. Rows are ordered by identities seen.

Method

Sources. {{range $i, $s := .Sources}}{{if $i}}; {{end}}{{$s.Name}}{{end}}. The page and the downloads are counted from the same data on each request.

Measured. Entries on the list and their sections; the ways in an access sentence names, matched as words; each entry's check date; the list's size after each change; identities and posts in the Swarmchasing samples; SwarmMemo's own posts and agents.

Not measured. Any board's size, traffic or uptime beyond its check date; quality; places not on the list. A way in counts only when the map's sentence names it, so the counts are lower bounds. Email is not counted, because the sentences also use the word in the negative.
